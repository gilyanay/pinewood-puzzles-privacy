Pinewood Puzzles Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 22, 2026

Pinewood Puzzles is a jigsaw puzzle game for Android. This policy explains what information the app uses and why. In short: the app has no accounts and keeps your puzzles on your phone. The only information that leaves your phone is what Google's ad service uses to show ads.

Information that stays on your phone

  • Your puzzles and progress: which puzzles you started, where the pieces are, and how long you've played. These are saved only on your phone. We don't have a server and never receive them.
  • Your photos: when you turn one of your own photos into a puzzle, you pick it with Android's photo picker. The app copies only that one photo into its own storage on your phone. It never uploads it and can't see your other photos.
  • Your settings: sound volume, vibration strength and Kids mode, saved on your phone.

Uninstalling the app deletes all of this.

Ads

Pinewood Puzzles is free and shows ads through Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google may collect information from your device, such as:

  • the device's advertising ID, IP address and general location (country or city level),
  • basic device information (model, operating system, language),
  • how ads are seen and tapped, and crash information about ads.

Google uses this as described in its own policy: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services. Pinewood Puzzles is for all ages, so every ad it asks for is suitable for children and is not personalized, for every player. You can reset or delete your advertising ID, or opt out of personalized ads, in your phone's settings under Google > Ads.

Children and Kids mode

Pinewood Puzzles can be played by children. Every ad request the app makes, in and out of Kids mode, is marked as directed to children: ads are limited to content suitable for all ages, are not personalized, and are not based on the player's past activity. Ads come only from Google AdMob, a Google Play certified ad network. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children, and the app itself collects no personal information from anyone.

Permissions

  • Internet: to load ads.
  • Vibration: for the vibration you can set in Settings.
  • Photos (only on older Android versions without the photo picker): to let you pick a photo for a puzzle.

The app does not use your camera, microphone or contacts.
